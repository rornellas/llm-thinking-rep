# Adversarial audit — pre-Qwen controlled transplant

**Grade:** `SUPPORTED_WITH_CORRECTIONS`

The recorded arithmetic and replay survive independent recomputation. The broad narrative requires corrections before it is used to authorize or block a larger experiment.

## Corrections

- **HIGH** — Only three independently trained teacher seeds are available. The crossed bootstrap is informative for the fixed seed/document population, but a seed-level t interval is wide and does not establish broad population significance.
- **HIGH** — The teacher loss was still decreasing at step 300. The controlled teacher was not demonstrably converged, so narrowing may receive a regularization advantage that is not representative of a mature pretrained MoE.
- **HIGH** — Modal K2 uses about 25.2% of full expert parameters while the d_ff=30 narrow comparator uses 75%. The failed equal-matrix-compute comparison does not refute a parameter-efficiency advantage; a Pareto frontier and parameter-matched comparator are required.
- **MEDIUM** — The sealed documents are generated by the same deterministic template family as training data. Seeds differ and exact/near duplicates were not observed, but this is distributional replication, not natural-language OOD validation.
- **MEDIUM** — A SHA-256 commitment proves that the revealed secret matches the preregistered digest. Local artifacts alone cannot externally attest that the training process never had access to the secret. The workflow design provides stronger isolation when executed in CI with job-scoped secrets.
- **MEDIUM** — The student was optimized for only 180 local-distillation steps. No convergence or budget sweep was part of the frozen comparison, so the failure conflates parameterization and optimization.

## Corrected conclusion

The scalar Modal K2 transplant is functionally viable on this controlled task and loses to a stronger, three-times-larger per-expert narrow model at equal dominant matrix arithmetic. This blocks a claim of equal-compute quality superiority, but does not yet rule out a useful parameter-memory Pareto advantage or improved parameterizations/optimization.
